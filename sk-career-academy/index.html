SK Career Academy No.1

Android app · coming soon on Google Play

The study app of SK Career Academy, Agra, a coaching centre for Class 9 to 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology and English). Made by Akash Kush for the academy.

What students get

  • Browse every class and its notes, and read free notes without signing up.
  • After admission at the academy, read your class's PDF notes and watch its videos inside the app.
  • Notes stay protected: view-only, watermarked, no downloads or screenshots.
  • Sign up with your mobile number (OTP) and log in with a password.
  • Follow the academy on YouTube, Telegram and WhatsApp.

For the academy

  • Upload PDFs and videos by class and subject, as free or for joined students.
  • Add students and manage their classes, plans and fee records.

Admission
