SK Career Academy No.1: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The SK Career Academy No.1 app for Android ("the app") is the study app of SK Career Academy, Agra, India, a coaching centre for Class 9 to 12. The app was developed and is published on Google Play by Akash Kush, an independent developer based in Agra, with the academy's permission. In this policy, "we" means SK Career Academy and its app developer.

The app lets anyone browse classes and read free notes, and lets students who have taken admission at the academy read their class's notes and videos.

1. Information we collect

  • Mobile number (required to sign up): to create your account, verify it by OTP and let you log in.
  • Password (required to sign up): to log in. It is stored securely by Google Firebase and is never visible to us.
  • Full name and class (required to sign up): to show your profile and the notes of your class.
  • Email, parent's mobile, school and address (optional): so the academy can contact you or your parents about your studies.
  • Profile photo (optional): taken with the front camera only if you tap "Take photo", and shown on your profile.
  • Admission details: which class you joined, your plan dates and, if you paid fees at the academy, the amount and date, so the academy can keep its records.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions, to show and measure ads.

Visitors who don't sign up can browse the app and read free notes without giving any personal information (apart from the advertising data above). We do not collect your contacts, files, SMS, call logs or precise location. The app has no in-app purchases; admission and fees are handled by the academy directly.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To give you access to the notes and videos of the class you joined.
  • So the academy can manage students and admissions and contact you about your studies.
  • To show ads that support the free parts of the app.
  • To keep the app working, safe and free of misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who can see your information

  • SK Career Academy (admin) can see student profiles, plans and fee records to run the academy.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) stores app data and files and handles login and OTP. Profile data is stored in India (Mumbai); notes, videos and profile photos are stored in Google Cloud Storage in the United States.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) shows ads in the app.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Google's handling of data is explained in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Students who haven't joined a class see a small banner and, now and then, a full-screen ad after closing a note. Students who have joined a class see only a small banner. Ads are never shown while you read a note or watch a video.

Because most of our students are 13 to 17 years old, the app asks Google to show only family-friendly ads and to apply its protections for teenage users. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Security

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Server access rules let each student see only their own profile, and let only students who joined a class open its notes.
  • Notes are shown inside the app only. They aren't saved on your phone, and screenshots and screen recording are blocked in the app.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your account information while your account exists. Fee records may be kept longer where the law requires accounting records to be kept. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below.

7. Delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile > Delete account and confirm. Your login, profile (name, class and optional details) and profile photo are deleted straight away.

Without the app: WhatsApp SK Career Academy on +91 99972 79436, or email akashkushtech.ai@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", from or mentioning the mobile number of your account. We will delete the same data within 30 days and confirm to you.

Fee records needed for accounting may be kept as the law requires; everything else is removed.

8. Children

The app is made for students of Class 9 to 12 (generally 13 to 17 years old) and their parents. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Parents can contact us at any time to see or delete their child's information.

9. Your rights

You can see and edit most of your details in the app under Profile > Edit details, and you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it using the contacts below, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
