# tech-akashkush.github.io

Portfolio of Akash Kush, the Focus Mode product page, its Terms of Service, and `app-ads.txt` for AdMob.

- Plain HTML and CSS, no build step. GitHub Pages serves the `main` branch.
- Icons in `icons.svg`: line icons from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC licence) and brand logos from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0). Brand names and logos are trademarks of their owners.
- Keep these URLs stable (Play Console and AdMob use them): `/`, `/focus-mode/`, `/focus-mode/terms/`, `/app-ads.txt`. The privacy policy lives in the separate repo `focus-mode-privacy`.
- SK Career Academy No.1 (Android app for SK Career Academy, Agra): `/sk-career-academy/` and its privacy policy `/sk-career-academy/privacy/` (account deletion at `/sk-career-academy/privacy/#delete-account`). Keep these URLs stable too (Play Console uses them).
