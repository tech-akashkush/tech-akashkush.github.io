1. What Focus Mode does

Focus Mode helps you limit distractions on your own phone. It can block apps, short videos (such as Reels and Shorts) and websites that you choose, run focus timers, apply schedules and daily limits, and show you statistics about your phone use. You decide what is blocked and when.

3. Strict mode and blocking

In a strict session you can't end or pause the session early. Please plan for this before you start one. Your phone's default calling app, the home screen and Android's Settings app are never blocked, so you can always make calls, including emergency calls.
